# Norwegian Humanizer

This reference adapts useful principles from `https://github.com/blader/humanizer` and the Marketing Playbook anti-slop rules for Norwegian business writing. Do not vendor or run the English-oriented humanizer directly; use the pattern categories manually.

## Remove generic AI openings

Replace:

- "I dagens digitale verden ..."
- "Kunstig intelligens er i ferd med å revolusjonere ..."
- "Det er ingen tvil om at ..."
- "I en tid der ..."
- "Mange bedrifter står overfor ..."

With a concrete observation:

- "Hver uke kommer det en ny AI-nyhet. De fleste ledere trenger ikke flere nyheter. De trenger å vite hva som faktisk endrer arbeidet."

## Cut vague intensity

Watch for:

- banebrytende
- revolusjonerende
- gamechanger
- sømløs
- kraftfull
- innovativ
- AI-drevet, unless it names what the AI actually does

Replace with the practical effect:

- saves time
- reduces manual work
- improves response quality
- makes a workflow possible
- changes who can do the work

## Avoid passive, agentless claims

Weak:

- "Det kan oppnås bedre resultater."
- "Prosesser kan automatiseres."
- "Data kan utnyttes mer effektivt."

Better:

- "En agent kan hente dataene, skrive første utkast og legge frem usikkerhetene for et menneske."
- "Lederen får en beslutning å vurdere, ikke en blank side å starte fra."

## Break AI rhythm

AI drafts often sound too symmetrical:

- three equally long bullets;
- every paragraph with the same length;
- repeated "ikke bare X, men Y";
- tidy transitions that add no meaning.
- audience labels repeated as if speaking about the reader instead of to them, such as "for norske ledere";
- confident but empty sentences such as "X er nøkkelen", "X peker på noe større", or "X åpner nye muligheter" without a concrete follow-up.

Fix by varying sentence length, merging obvious lists, and keeping only transitions that help the reader.

## Cut filler sentences

Delete sentences that only restate the heading or add a corporate pause:

- "Det er her Fable 5 peker på noe større."
- "Dette er relevant for flere enn teknologimiljøer."
- "Det handler til syvende og sist om verdi."

Replace them with proof, example, or action:

- "Test den på en oppgave der mennesker i dag bruker timer på å samle, sjekke og skrive førsteutkast."
- "Hvis agenten ikke kan lese kildene selv, blir den bare en dyr tekstgenerator."

## Keep Norwegian natural

- Prefer everyday Norwegian over translated English.
- Use "arbeid", "oppgave", "verktøy", "tilgang", "kilder", and "beslutning" when those words are enough.
- Explain English product terms once, then use them consistently.
- A slightly human sentence is better than a perfectly polished corporate one.

## Final read-aloud check

Ask:

- Would a busy leader understand this without following AI news every day?
- Does every technical word earn its place?
- Does the article say something concrete enough that a competitor could disagree?
- Is there at least one useful sentence the reader could repeat in a meeting?
- Did the edit remove enough text that the article became faster to read?
